Set up in about three minutes

The translator uses Claude, an AI made by a company called Anthropic. It needs its own key, like a password that only you have. This guide walks you through getting one, step by step. You do this once and never again.

Step 1 of 4: Create your account

Tap the button below to open Anthropic's website. Sign up there; the easiest way is to tap Continue with Google and use your Gmail. Then come back here.

Open console.anthropic.com

This browser would not open the site in a new tab. Open this address in any other browser window, then come back here:

console.anthropic.com

It opens in a new tab; this guide stays right here, waiting for you.

Step 2 of 4: Add a card

Once you are signed in: click your name in the bottom-left corner, choose Organization settings, then Billing, and add a payment card. On a phone, if you do not see a side panel, tap the menu button (three lines) at the top first.

While you are in Billing, look for Limits and set a monthly limit of $5. Translating costs pennies, and with the limit it can never surprise you.

Step 3 of 4: Create your key

In the left side panel, click API keys, then the Create key button. Name it anything, "translator" is fine. Click Create, then Copy. Your new key is now copied and ready to paste.

Step 4 of 4: Paste it here

Paste the key you just copied. It stays on this phone and is sent only to Anthropic.

You're all set

Your key works and is saved on this phone. Type English and you get natural Israeli Hebrew with the vowel marks double-checked by a specialist. Tap the Share button in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen to keep it like a real app.

Settings

The app talks directly to Claude, Anthropic's AI, for translation. It needs an API key once. The key is stored only on this device, never sent anywhere except to Anthropic.

The double-check sends just the Hebrew sentence to Nakdan, a free tool from Dicta, an Israeli research nonprofit whose vowel-pointing is the best there is. If it spots a wrong mark, the app corrects itself.
